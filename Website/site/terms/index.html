Accuracy of the chemistry

Elemora's element data follows the IUPAC 2021 standard atomic weights, and its compound records are verified against PubChem and carry the PubChem compound identifier. Structures drawn in the app are representative depictions computed from real connectivity, and the app labels each one for what it is. A composition the app cannot match to a known compound is marked as a hypothetical composition and is never given an invented name, structure or property. Even so, Elemora is a study aid: it is not a laboratory reference, and it must not be relied on for safety decisions, dosing, synthesis or any professional purpose.
