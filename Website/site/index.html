Elemora

The periodic table, learned.

Elemora is a periodic table and chemistry study app for iPhone and iPad. All 118 elements with real data, interactive structures, a compound catalog and a study system that remembers what you keep getting wrong.

What is in it

  • Every element, with IUPAC 2021 atomic weights and sourced properties.
  • Atomic and molecular structures you can turn, zoom and take apart.
  • A verified compound catalog, plus PubChem lookups for anything else.
  • Flashcards, quizzes, Match, Identify and Smart Review.
  • Quizzes you can shape, save and share as a link.

Elemora Pro

Elemora is free to use: the whole periodic table, search, compounds, the builder and three study rounds a day cost nothing. Elemora Pro adds unlimited study, Smart Review, the 3D structure explorer for every element and full depth on all 118.

Elemora Pro is an auto-renewing subscription, offered monthly and yearly and billed through your Apple Account. Prices are shown in the app in your own currency before you buy anything. Manage or cancel it in Settings on your device.
