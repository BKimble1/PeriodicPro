Privacy Policy

Last updated 17 September 2026. This policy covers the Elemora iOS app and this website.

Elemora has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking of any kind. Everything you do in the app is stored on your device.

What Elemora stores, and where

The app keeps the following on your device only, in its own storage:

  • Your progress: familiarity scores per element and per compound, your streak, and how many study rounds you have completed today.
  • Your favorites and the compounds you have added to your study material.
  • Quizzes you have created or saved, including any opened from a shared link.
  • Your recent searches, and compounds looked up from PubChem, cached so they work offline.
  • Your appearance preference (System, Light or Dark).
  • Which study reminders you have turned on and the time you chose for them.

None of this is uploaded anywhere. There is no Elemora account, no login, no sign-in with anything, and no server that holds a copy. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

PubChem lookups

When you search for a compound or build one the app does not already know, Elemora sends that name or molecular formula to PubChem, a public chemistry database run by the U.S. National Library of Medicine, to retrieve the record. Only the term you looked up is sent. No identifier, no device information and nothing about you travels with it, and no request is made unless you search or build something. The result is cached on your device so it works offline afterwards. PubChem's own privacy practices are theirs; see nlm.nih.gov.

The chemistry scanner

Scan Chemistry uses your camera to read chemical names, molecular formulas and structure identifiers off a page. Camera frames are processed on your device and are never recorded, saved or uploaded. Recognition runs inside Apple's on-device Vision framework; Elemora keeps no image, writes none to disk and sends none anywhere. There is no third-party image-recognition service in the app.

The only thing that can leave your device is the text it read — a name, a formula, a SMILES string, an InChI or an InChIKey — and only when the app's own catalog and your cache do not already have it. That text goes to PubChem exactly like a typed search. Camera access is asked for only when you open Scan, never at launch, and declining leaves the rest of the app working. Elemora never asks for access to your photos.

Notifications

Study reminders are optional and off until you turn them on. They are created on your device: there is no notification server, no push account and no token. Nothing about what you study is sent anywhere to produce one. Permission is asked for only when you turn a reminder on in Settings, never at launch, and turning the master switch off removes every reminder Elemora has pending. At most one arrives a day, and none of them is marked time-sensitive or critical, so a Focus silences them.

The Home Screen widget

Elemora's widgets are optional — one exists only if you add it — and neither of them touches the network. A widget draws from a small file the app writes into an app group container, which is a folder the app and its widget can both open on your device: the questions it may ask, and the four numbers it shows. Answers you give on the Home Screen are written to the same folder and merged into your progress the next time you open Elemora, counting for the day you gave them. The folder is not cloud storage, is not synced, is not shared with any other app, and is deleted with the app.

Subscriptions

Elemora Pro is sold through Apple's In-App Purchase system. Apple handles the payment and tells the app only whether a subscription is currently active. Elemora never sees your payment details, your Apple Account, your name or your email address. Apple's handling of that transaction is covered by Apple's own privacy policy.

Shared quiz links

A shared quiz link carries the quiz's name and its settings, encoded into the link itself. Nothing is uploaded: there is no server that stores the quiz, and the link is the quiz. No progress, no favorites, no history and no identifier of the person who made it is included. Opening someone's link saves the quiz on your device and changes nothing else.

This website

These pages are plain static files. They set no cookies, run no analytics, load no fonts, scripts or images from anywhere else, and embed nothing from a third party. The host that serves them keeps ordinary server logs, which it uses to deliver and protect the site.

Children

Elemora is a study app suitable for general audiences. It collects no personal information from anybody, of any age.

Your rights

Because Elemora holds no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to export, correct or delete on request. The data the app stores is in your own hands: Reset Progress in Settings clears your familiarity scores and streak, and deleting the app removes everything.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version is published here with a new date. Material changes will also be described in the app's release notes.
