Common questions

How do I restore a subscription on a new device?

Open Settings from the gear on the Progress tab and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account that bought it.

How do I cancel Elemora Pro?

Settings in Elemora has a Manage Subscription row, which opens Apple's own subscription settings. That is the only place a subscription can be changed or cancelled; Elemora cannot do it for you.

Someone sent me a quiz link and nothing happened.

An Elemora quiz link opens the app when Elemora is installed. If it opened this website instead, Elemora is not installed on that device yet — or the link was opened somewhere that strips the app association, in which case copying it into Safari or Messages and tapping it there usually works.

A compound search says it could not reach PubChem.

Online compound lookups need a connection. The bundled catalog and anything you have looked up before keep working offline.

Will I lose my progress if I delete the app?

Yes. Everything Elemora stores is on your device and there is no account to sync it to, so deleting the app deletes your progress, favorites and saved quizzes.

How do I reset my progress?

Settings, then Reset Progress. Familiarity scores and your streak are cleared; your favorites are kept.
