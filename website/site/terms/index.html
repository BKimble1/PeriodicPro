What Elemora is

Elemora is an educational reference and study app about the chemical elements. It presents the periodic table, element data and written explanations, lets you practise with flashcards, quizzes and identification drills, tracks what you have studied, and lets you assemble formulas and look at compound structures.

It is a learning tool. It is not a laboratory instrument, a measurement device, a safety data sheet, a regulatory reference, a dispensing or dosage aid, or a substitute for professional, medical, academic or scientific judgement.

Educational use and the limits of the data

We work to make Elemora's chemistry accurate and to describe it clearly, but we do not warrant that every value, explanation, diagram, structure or classification in the app is complete, current or correct. Published values differ between sources, are revised over time, and are often rounded or simplified for teaching.

Elemora says so where it matters. Its shell diagrams are labelled as a simplified model, with a note that electrons do not travel on fixed circular paths. Its bonding hints are labelled not a verification: they are there to help you think, and they do not certify that a structure you have built is chemically valid, stable, safe or real.

Do not rely on Elemora for anything where being wrong matters. It must not be used as the basis for laboratory work, handling or storing chemicals, safety decisions, medical or veterinary decisions, industrial processes, regulatory compliance, or graded academic work submitted as fact. Check a primary source, your instructor, or the relevant safety documentation.

Nothing in Elemora is an instruction or an encouragement to synthesise, obtain, handle or combine any substance.
