Already have Elemora?

Go back to the message, email or note the link arrived in and tap it there. Elemora opens, checks the quiz and saves it to My Quizzes.

  1. Tap the link where you received it

    Opening it from Messages, Mail, Notes or AirDrop hands the link to Elemora directly. If you are reading this page, the link opened in a browser instead — which is what happens when Elemora is not installed yet, or when you have previously chosen to open elemora.idlery.com in Safari.

  2. Or touch and hold the link, then choose Open in Elemora

    iOS offers that on a link it knows belongs to an installed app. It is the reliable way back into the app once a link has already opened in a browser.

  3. Elemora does the rest

    The quiz is checked, saved under My Quizzes, and a confirmation appears with its title, what it covers, and buttons to start it or to see it in My Quizzes. Opening the same link twice does not create a second copy.

A browser cannot force a link to open in an app, so there is no button here that can do it for you. The link stays valid for as long as you keep the message — it does not expire, and it does not have to be re-sent.

Just installed Elemora?

Return to this message and tap the shared link again. Installing an app does not hand it the link you tapped beforehand, so that one tap is the whole of it.

1. Install Elemora

Get the app on your iPhone or iPad from the App Store.

2. Tap the link again

Go back to the original message and tap the same link. Nothing has to be re-sent.

3. The quiz is saved

Elemora opens on the confirmation, with the quiz already in My Quizzes.

What is actually in this link

The quiz, and nothing else
A shared link carries the quiz's name and its settings — which elements or compounds it draws from, how many questions, and the question types. That is all. No study history, no favourites, no scores, and nothing that identifies the sender or their device.
Nothing is stored on this website
There is no Elemora account and no quiz database. The quiz is encoded into the address itself, which is why it works without anyone signing in — and why the same link opens the same quiz on any device.
Encoded is not encrypted
Treat a shared quiz link like any other link you send: whoever receives or forwards the message can open it, and it can appear in browser history, in the logs of the services that carry it, and in link-preview fetches. Share it with people you mean to share it with.

More detail is in the Privacy Policy. This page loads no scripts, sets no cookies and sends the quiz nowhere.
